Install the Chrome extension (one time, 2 minutes)
  1. In Chrome, open a new tab and go to chrome://extensions
  2. Turn on Developer mode (switch at the top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder GTM.SOVE7GN.ORG\extension on your laptop.
  4. Click the puzzle icon next to the address bar and pin SOVE7GN LinkedIn Bot.
  5. Click the bot’s icon, paste the bot key from GTM.SOVE7GN.ORG\_research\LI_BOT_KEY.txt, press Save.
  6. Make sure you’re signed in to LinkedIn in that Chrome, then press Test LinkedIn, then Run now.
  7. Leave Chrome open. The bot keeps a pinned LinkedIn tab of its own; don’t close it.
